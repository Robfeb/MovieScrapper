Synopsis
During 13 days of October 1940, Ramona Companys, Lluís Companys sister; Ramón de Colubi, his defense attorney; and Luis Orgaz, Captain General of Catalunya, try to avoid, in different ways and for different reasons, that the President of the Generalitat is executed and thus becomes a martyr.
